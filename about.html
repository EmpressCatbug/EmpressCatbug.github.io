ABOUT ME

There’s more to me than what I build.

I’m an engineer, creator, lifelong learner, and person of faith. Curiosity pushes me to understand how things work, creativity challenges me to imagine what they could become, and the people and values I care about keep me grounded along the way.

01 / MY STORY

Curiosity has a habit of becoming a project.

I’ve always been drawn to learning by doing. When something catches my interest, I tend to go down the rabbit hole—reading, watching, researching, experimenting, and trying to understand as much of it as I can.

Learning something conceptually is only part of the process for me. I want to put it into practice. Sometimes that means finding an exercise to work through, and other times it means creating an entire project simply because I want somewhere to apply what I’m learning.

That mindset is one of the things that draws me to software engineering. Technology is constantly changing. There will always be another problem to understand, another tool to explore, or another skill worth developing—and I genuinely enjoy that.

HOW I LEARN

Explore deeply. Practice deliberately. Build something. Learn from what didn’t work. Then keep going.

02 / CREATIVITY
MAKING IDEAS TANGIBLE

I like turning “what if?” into something real.

Creativity, to me, is the process of taking an abstract idea and finding a way to bring it into the real world. I enjoy building the foundation first and then refining the details until the result feels intentional, useful, and visually complete.

That mindset shows up in a lot of places in my life. I enjoy imagining stories and games, designing websites, experimenting with visual ideas, getting creative in the kitchen, and looking for approaches that might not be the most obvious solution.

02 BUILD

03 REFINE

04 POLISH

03 / FAITH
THE FOUNDATION BENEATH EVERYTHING

Learning to trust beyond my own strength.

I first came to faith in Jesus as a child. Growing up in the church, I came to know God as my Savior, healer, provider, and the foundation of my faith. At six years old, I was baptized alongside my mom, which remains a special memory for me.

As I grew into adulthood, however, I found myself increasingly relying on my own strength. I tried to control what I could, carried fears on my own, and began placing limits on what I trusted God to handle. Over time, that left me feeling spiritually lost and searching for hope in places that could not truly provide it.

Returning more intentionally to my faith has meant learning to surrender fear, anxiety, perfectionism, and the need for control. I am learning to trust God with every part of my life rather than only the parts I feel comfortable releasing.

My childhood baptism is a memory I treasure. My adult baptism was a decision I made intentionally for myself.

Choosing baptism again as an adult became a public declaration of my faith and my desire to continue growing closer to Christ. My faith shapes the values I try to carry into the rest of my life: kindness, compassion, humility, perseverance, generosity, and the way I treat the people around me.

04 / COMMUNITY
FINDING A CHURCH HOME

Faith grows differently when it is lived in community.

My connection with Buckhead Church began through my partner, who attended there as a child and later returned as an adult. We first began watching services online together before eventually attending in person.

Since then, I have been trying to become more involved rather than simply attending services. That has included participating in Bible study and small groups, meeting other people in the church community, and creating more intentional space for faith in everyday life.

Buckhead Church also became the place where I chose to be baptized again as an adult, making it an important part of this chapter of my walk with Christ.
