ABOUT ME

There’s more to me than what I build.

I’m an engineer, creator, lifelong learner, and person of faith. Curiosity pushes me to understand how things work, creativity challenges me to imagine what they could become, and the people and values I care about keep me grounded along the way.

CURIOSITY CREATIVITY ✦ FAITH GROWTH
01 / MY STORY

Curiosity has a habit of becoming a project.

I’ve always been drawn to learning by doing. When something catches my interest, I tend to go down the rabbit hole—reading, watching, researching, experimenting, and trying to understand as much of it as I can.

Learning something conceptually is only part of the process for me. I want to put it into practice. Sometimes that means finding an exercise to work through, and other times it means creating an entire project simply because I want somewhere to apply what I’m learning.

That mindset is one of the things that draws me to software engineering. Technology is constantly changing. There will always be another problem to understand, another tool to explore, or another skill worth developing—and I genuinely enjoy that.

HOW I LEARN

Explore deeply. Practice deliberately. Build something. Learn from what didn’t work. Then keep going.
